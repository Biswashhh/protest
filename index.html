Genz Protest

The Gen-Z protest in Nepal on the day of 8th and 9th September, 2025 was a student led protest against corruption and injustice by the government.
The protest was done against those who are responsible for developing nation were making personal benefit
and living luxury life where as some citizens where having hard time just for food 2 times a day.

Many students in school dress got killed by the police and curfew in whole country was applied and
the protest stopped as the resignation and removal or parliaments committe. The day is set as a National Holiday named as "Gen-Z Day"
in memories of those who sacrificed for the nation that day.
